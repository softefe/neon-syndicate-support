Privacy Policy — Cyberpunk Neon Syndicate: Tycoon

Effective Date: November 10, 2026

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

Cyberpunk Neon Syndicate: Tycoon (com.neonsyndicate.tycoon), developed by Efe Sarioglu, does not collect, store, track, or share any personal data, device identifiers, location data, or usage analytics.

2. Local Game Save Storage

All game progression (Digital Credits, Bit-Chips, Street Rep, District Control, Fixer Contracts, and Mercenary Crew) is stored locally on your iOS device using on-device storage (AsyncStorage).

3. In-App Purchases

Optional In-App Purchases are processed securely through Apple StoreKit / App Store Connect and RevenueCat solely to validate anonymous transaction receipts and unlock purchased digital content. We never have access to your payment card or billing details.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact Efe Sarioglu at sarioglu_efe45@icloud.com.
