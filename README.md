## Technical Support & FAQ

### How do I play Cyberpunk Neon Syndicate: Tycoon?
1. **Main Grid (District & Fixer Ops):** Accept multi-stage branching Fixer Contracts from Mama Kestrel, Ozone, Saint Patch, and Vesper Lin. Choose how to resolve each encounter using your Netrunners, Cyber-Guards, or Fixers to earn Street Rep and expand control over Neo-Veridia's four districts (*Neon Bazaar*, *Rustwater Docks*, *The Undercity*, and *Helix Arcology*).
2. **Tactical (ICE Breach & Hex-Splice):** Launch timed corporate breaches and play the interactive **Hex-Splice Override** mini-game by tapping the target hexadecimal sequence in order for an instant firewall breach and +25% bonus payout.
3. **Black Market (Operatives & Servers):** Recruit Netrunners, Cyber-Guards (who reduce Corp Heat gain), and Fixers, and upgrade quantum server blades for passive credit flow.
4. **Cyber Bar (StoreKit Implants & Bit-Chips):** Purchase Bit-Chips or permanent implants (*Neon Cyber-Overlord* and *AI Netrunner Bot*) or tap **Restore Purchases** at any time.
