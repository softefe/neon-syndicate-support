Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my non-consumable implants (Neon Cyber-Overlord / AI Netrunner Bot)?
Open the Cyber Bar tab inside the app and tap Restore Purchases at the bottom of the screen while signed in with the Apple ID used for the original purchase.

How do I reduce Corp Heat?
Corp Heat decays passively over time, and recruiting Chrome-Plate Cyber-Guards in the Black Market reduces all heat gained from contracts and failed ICE splices by 5% per guard (up to 50%).
